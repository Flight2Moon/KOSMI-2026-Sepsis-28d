Nominal significance An unadjusted 95% paired bootstrap confidence interval for Δ must exclude zero. The analysis used 1,000 patient-level resamples without multiplicity correction.

Reading the results

Absolute differences were below 0.02 in 23 comparisons. The two larger differences were both observed with Decision Tree, in eICU-CRD primary and SICdb. The only nominal two-stage advantage occurred with Decision Tree in MIMIC-IV temporal evaluation.

Ten intervals favored multiclass, one favored two-stage, and 14 included zero. The 0.02 cutoff describes the observed differences. Equivalence was not tested.

Study context

The primary metric was macro AUROC for three possible 28-day hospital outcomes. XGBoost achieved a direct multiclass macro AUROC of 0.832 in development, 0.789 in temporal evaluation, and 0.739–0.782 in external evaluation. The study found no consistent discrimination benefit from the death-first approach.

The analysis used frozen cohort labels. The public release does not reproduce all original Sepsis-3 extraction steps.
